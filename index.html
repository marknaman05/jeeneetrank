The problem with predictors

A lookup table can't answer the question you're actually asking

Every free predictor in the market runs the same query: compare your rank to last year's closing rank, print a list. That was never the hard part.

01

A list isn't an order

You submit 100–300 preferences in sequence. An alphabetised set of eligible colleges leaves the entire decision — what goes at #1 versus #40 — still on you.

02

"High chance" is not a number

A colour-coded chip is a threshold on a spreadsheet column. It can't tell you the odds that #12 is where you actually end up — or whether your list runs out before it catches you.

03

It forgets you immediately

Counselling runs six weeks and five-plus rounds. Freeze, float, slide, withdraw, mop-up, stray vacancy — each with a deadline and a forfeited deposit. Every tool is one page visit.

How it works

We run the allotment, not a spreadsheet

We execute the real seat-allocation algorithm against the real seat matrix, ten thousand times, across a distribution of how ranks move year to year. You get, at every position in your list, the probability that it's the seat you're allotted — and whether the list has enough safety at the bottom.

Questions

The ones you should be asking

How accurate is this, really?

We publish a calibration scorecard after every season: what we predicted, what happened, where we were wrong. Nobody else in this category will show you their error rate. Ours is on the record.

Where does the data come from?

Official published seat matrices and round-wise allotment data from JoSAA, CSAB, MCC and the state counselling bodies. The same public source everyone uses — the difference is entirely in what we do with it.

Will I get calls from colleges?

Not from us. We don't sell, share or broker your contact details, and we take no money from any college. That is the whole reason there is a price on this page.

Do you cover state counselling?

Central counselling first, done properly. State bodies are added one at a time with their full quota rules modelled — not scraped cutoffs with the reservation logic guessed at. We'd rather cover three states honestly than thirty badly.

Is this the same as a counselling consultant?

No. We give you the maths and the deadlines; you submit everything yourself on the official portal. We will never offer to arrange a seat, and you should walk away from anyone who does.
